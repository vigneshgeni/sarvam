Sarvam design v3.

Every screen of the app, in flow order. Open (playable) runs the real motion and the transitions between screens (tap links inside a screen). Videos loop each animated screen. The PNGs and sarvam-design-v3.pdf are the pixel target at 390 px.

Prototype tour: onboarding → Home → Reading A → Result (cards fade up, 40 ms stagger) → Share sheet (slides up 260 ms) → Save as image → close (sheet slides down 200 ms) → Home → health checkup (one upload, three documents).

Push: 280 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), 28 px slide + fade. Back: mirror. Leaving screen: 160 ms fade. Sheets: scrim fades 200 ms, sheet rises 260 ms. Reduced motion: everything instant.
